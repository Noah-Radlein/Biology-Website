General Characteristics

Fungi are eukaryotic organisms as their cells contain a membrane-bound nucleus and membrane bound organelles and are distinct from plants and animals.

Cell Structure

While Fungi have cell walls, they are made of chitin, a large chain of sugar molecules, instead of cellulose. This is one of the key differences that separates plants from fungi.


Most Fungi are multicellular and made of long thread like structures called hyphae. These hyphae form a network called the mycelium, which is the main body of the fungus.
