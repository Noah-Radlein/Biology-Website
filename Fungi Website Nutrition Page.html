How Fungi Obtain Food

Fungi are primarily heterotrophic, meaning they can't create their own food and there are currently no known species of fungi that are autotrophs. Fungi nutrition comes from 3 channels which are saprotrophism, parasitism and symbiotic relationships. They release digestive enzymes into their surroundings, break down complex substances, and absorb simpler nutrients.

  • Saprophytic: Feed on dead matter (e.g., mushrooms).
  • Parasitic: Feed on living hosts (e.g., Candida).
  • Mutualistic: Form partnerships with plants (mycorrhizae).
